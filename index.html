I THE ARRIVAL

All humans from the world died. But after death, they found themselves in a place that felt surreal and different—a realm smaller than the world they knew, bound by a 10,000 block radius.

In this Afterlife, humans felt more powerful, filled with an ethereal essence. They wandered and discovered that this world offered wonders beyond imagination.

But there was a mysterious entity lurking in the shadows: The Void. Whispers spread of a being offering power in exchange for souls.

II THE CORRUPTION

A divine being appeared, giving the humans a book with a warning: "Beware the Void." But blinded by power, many chose to trade their essence for strength.

With each death—whether accidental, intentional, or in battle—their soul was traded to the Void. This granted immense power but came with a price: corruption.

The corrupted fought amongst themselves while searching for a cure. Many died completely, their souls fully consumed by the Void, which laughed from the shadows.

THE VOID

An incomprehensible entity that feeds on souls. Offers power in exchange for essence, corrupting everything it touches.

THE DIVINE BEING

A celestial entity who warned humanity about the Void. Provides guidance through ancient texts.

VOID-TAINED

Players who have traded too much of their soul. Glowing with void energy, they walk a fine line between power and oblivion.

III THE AWAKENING

Void-born forces awaken one by one. Guided by a prophet, players confront the Child of the Second Death, uncovering lore that leads to the Abyss Firstborn.

Its defeat plunges the entire world into endless night as nightmare mobs and corruption spread across the SMP. As time runs out and parasites invade, players face the Reaper from the Darkside.

Finally, they encounter the Void itself—an incomprehensible, near-unkillable entity tied to the corrupted Ender Dragon and the collapse of the Afterlife.

IV THE AFTERMATH

The Void is defeated, but its fall is suspicious—not by players' hands. This sets up Chapter 2, where void-tainted players, fallen souls, and banned players' accumulated rage manifests into a new being.

Abyss Reborn—meant to destroy the Void—instead becomes a greater threat against survival itself. Who has the power to change the story? Who killed the Void? Is there a greater threat lying unseen?

The corruption spreads, and the true enemy remains hidden in plain sight...

Stage 1: Ethereal (0-2 deaths) - Your soul arrives pure. Full 20 hearts. The Void's whispers are distant.

Stage 2: Altered (3-5 deaths) - Gain permanent Speed I. Max hearts reduced to 18.

Stage 3: Mutated (6-8 deaths) - Gain Strength I, Jump Boost I, but constant Hunger. Max hearts reduced to 16.

Stage 4: Corrupted (9 deaths) - Gain Strength I, Speed I, Hunger, but Mining Fatigue slows work. Max hearts reduced to 14.

Stage 5: Forsaken (10 deaths) - Ultimate power with Strength II, Resistance, Night Vision, but constant Hunger II and Slowness. Glowing makes you visible to all. Max hearts reduced to 12.

Stage 6: Void-Tained (11 deaths) - Permanent ban. Your soul is consumed by the Void forever.

Kill Bonus: Forsaken players gain +½ heart per player kill (max +3 hearts). This allows skilled Forsaken to reach up to 15 hearts through combat.

THE DIVINE MANDATES

1

NO HACKED CLIENTS

Any form of cheating, hacking, or using unauthorized modifications is strictly prohibited. This includes X-ray texture packs, fly hacks, speed hacks, or any client that provides an unfair advantage.

2

RESPECT ALL PLAYERS

Treat others with respect. No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks. Swearing is allowed but keep it reasonable and not directed at individuals.

3

NO EXCESSIVE PVP

PvP is allowed, but don't make the server PvP-based. No spawn camping, repeated killing of the same player, or forcing PvP on unwilling participants. 1-2 kills is fine; targeting players repeatedly is not.

4

NO ADVERTISING

Do not advertise other servers, websites, or services in chat, signs, or builds. This includes Discord servers not related to AscensionSMP.

5

NO BEGGING

Do not beg for items, ranks, OP, or special treatment. Earn everything through gameplay. Feedback is welcome, but don't demand features.

SERVER SPECIFICS

6

NO WORLD GUARD

There is no land protection. Griefing is allowed. If you build something valuable, hide it well or defend it. This is a true survival experience.

7

NO TEAM PLUGINS

Team up vanilla-style. No team teleportation, shared homes, or team plugins. Form alliances organically through trust and cooperation.

8

REPORTING ISSUES

If you have problems, report to moderators in Discord. Do not ping the owner directly unless absolutely necessary. Moderators are fast tunnels for issues.

BAN APPEAL PROCESS

If you believe you were banned unfairly:

  1. Join the Discord server
  2. Message a moderator with your Minecraft username
  3. Explain what happened and why you think the ban was unfair
  4. Wait for a response (usually within 48 hours)
  5. If the appeal is accepted, you'll be unbanned with a warning
  6. Repeat offenses result in permanent bans

Note: Void-Tained players (11 deaths) cannot appeal their ban. Their souls have been consumed by the Void permanently.
